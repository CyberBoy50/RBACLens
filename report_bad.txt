Defensive lesson — least privilege in RBAC: give every subject only the permissions it needs, nothing more. A binding to cluster-admin hands over the whole cluster: read every secret, run code in any pod, change RBAC itself. Scope roles to exact verbs, resources and namespaces instead.

Lab use only — audit only manifests or clusters you own or have written permission to audit.
